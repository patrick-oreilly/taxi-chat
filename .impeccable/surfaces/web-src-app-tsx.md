# Surface: Taxi Chat app screen

Mode: Operate. A single working screen: ask a question, watch it get answered or declined, see how.
Audience/job: Patrick recording a demo; viewers judging whether every number is traceable.
Constraints: pinned direction (night city, taxi-yellow, Geist, dark glass); code-led; recording at 1920×1080, screenshots at 1440.

## Direction contract

seed: none (direction pinned by the owner; concept-seed not run)

THESIS: The city answers. The map is the answer surface, not decoration beside a chat box; refuses the category default of a centred chat column with a map thumbnail.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-black night ground (#07090d), streets as faint cool-grey zone lines, sodium/taxi yellow (#F6B800 family) as the only lit colour, used for answers. Dark smoked-glass panels with hairline borders. Geist for language, Geist Mono for numbers, SQL and confidences only.

STORY: Ask → the trace streams in on the right (Jev's read, resolved details, query, checks) → the answer lands in the conversation and the city lights up the zones it names. A decline dims the whole city and says why.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark NYC map with 265 zone outlines; left glass rail with the conversation, suggested questions and input; right glass rail "How I got this"; top-left wordmark, top-right exact trip count; bottom-right rows-scanned counter.

SIGNATURE: Highlighted zones rise as lit extruded blocks (height from the answer's value, rank order staggered), like a skyline switching its lights on.

RISK: Glow and glass drifting into neon costume; keep yellow scarce and meaningful.
